Radargram flyby with bad PRF vs four-PRF switching

The grayscale background is a public NASA Operation IceBridge/CReSIS Greenland radargram. The red overlays are a straight-line flyby aliasing experiment placed on top of it to show how a wrong fixed PRF can make one continuous blur layer, while a four-PRF schedule moves the folded clutter into separate blur dots.
