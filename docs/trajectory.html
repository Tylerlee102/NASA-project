SPICE reference-trajectory geometry

The spacecraft path is sampled from the public reference SPK around the October 23, 2031 encounter. The 12 surface points and subsurface target remain synthetic and fixed in Europa's body frame.

2

Adaptive four-PRF schedule

Each 100 ms decision block prioritizes zero predicted overlaps, then maximizes the worst range-wrapped Doppler–depth margin over its following nine 10 ms ticks.

3

Current block candidate scores

All four PRFs are evaluated against the same predicted SPICE geometry. The longest bar is selected for this block.

4

Selected-PRF Doppler × depth

The current adaptive PRF wraps Doppler and relative fast-time range for every modeled clutter point. Interference requires entry into the rectangular target basket in both axes.

5

Fixed bad PRF

One closest-approach fold-tuned PRF is reused for the whole flyby. Doppler and range-wrapped branches are retained, producing the continuous curved and crossing clutter pattern.

6

Adaptive best-of-four PRF

Block-level PRF changes break and displace the folded clutter branches while the SPICE-derived target trace remains continuous.

7

Illustrative rejection mask—not processed I/Q

The display fades PRF-dependent branches and highlights the target to show the proposed rejection concept. It is explicitly not matched filtering, range migration, coherent integration, or mission-validated focusing.
