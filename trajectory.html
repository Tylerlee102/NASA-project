Europa flyby with PRF folding

Move the satellite along the curved altitude profile while the surface clutter and subsurface target remain fixed.

Model boundary: NASA publishes flybys as low as 25 km and an early mission study uses 4.5 km/s as a typical flyby speed. This page uses those values with Europa's 1,560.8 km radius in a local straight-line tangent pass. It is not an operational SPICE reconstruction. The effective PRF is intentionally tuned to demonstrate folding and is not claimed to be NASA's flight PRF. NASA mission FAQ NASA flyby-speed study NASA Europa radius
