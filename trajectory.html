SPICE reference-trajectory geometry

The spacecraft path is sampled from the public reference SPK around the October 23, 2031 encounter. The 12 surface points and subsurface target remain synthetic and fixed in Europa's body frame.

2

Adaptive four-PRF schedule

Each 100 ms block uses the candidate that maximizes the worst predicted Doppler–depth separation over its following nine 10 ms ticks.

3

Current block candidate scores

All four PRFs are evaluated against the same predicted SPICE geometry. The longest bar is selected for this block.

4

Selected-PRF Doppler × depth

The current adaptive PRF is applied to every modeled clutter point. Interference requires entry into the target basket in both axes.

5

Fixed bad PRF

One closest-approach fold-tuned PRF is reused for the whole flyby. Every modeled nonzero fold branch is retained, producing the continuous curved and crossing clutter pattern.

6

Adaptive best-of-four PRF

Block-level PRF changes break and displace the folded clutter branches while the SPICE-derived target trace remains continuous.

7

Illustrative multi-PRF postprocessing

PRF-dependent clutter is attenuated as inconsistent across blocks; the PRF-independent subsurface target trace is retained. This is a processing concept, not mission-validated focusing.
